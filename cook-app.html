Pricing

Cost basis: off uses each ingredient's fixed calc price. On uses the weighted-average price you've actually paid (Trade Log), falling back to calc price for ingredients you haven't bought. Price = (ingredients + labor) ÷ yield × markup × demand.

Trade-in (Buyback)

When a buyer brings ingredients at the Counter, they land in your pantry and knock coin off the bill. Set a Par per ingredient (Pantry tab) to split the rate.
