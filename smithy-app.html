Items

Pricing

Markups are per-customer →
When on, sale prices round to whole numbers; off shows the exact price. Costs always show exact values.
Cost basis: off, every cost, inventory value and profit uses each material's fixed calc price. On, it uses the weighted-average price you've actually paid in the Inventory trade log (falling back to the calc price for materials you haven't bought yet).

Customers

Each customer is one markup — lower it for people you charge less. Chosen on the Craft page. The first row (Standard) sets the price shown in the Database. Buyback is the credit rate for parts they bring — blank inherits the shop default.
