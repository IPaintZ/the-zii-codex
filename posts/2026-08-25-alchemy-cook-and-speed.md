# Keizaal Workshops — Update: two new workshops + a big speed pass

Since the last post, the Keizaal Workshops calculator has grown from **three tools to five**, and the whole suite got a lot snappier.

---

## 🆕 Two new workshops

**⚗ Alchemist's Lab** — price out potions, poisons and reagents from the *KzlCraftingAlchemy* station. Browse by effect (Restore Health, Fortify Magicka, Cure Disease…), pick a tier, and see cost, sale price and margin per brew — plus a brew queue with running batch profit. 46 recipes and 43 ingredients pulled straight from the mod.

**🍲 Cook** — the cookbook. 39 dishes across cookpot and oven stations, with a pantry, buyer list, a serving tray and a sales view. Set your ingredient prices once and every dish reprices automatically.

Both sit alongside the existing **⚒ Smithy**, **🧵 Tailor** and **⚖️ Traders** tools. As with the others, your entered prices, stock and settings save in *your own browser* — nothing is shared, nothing leaves your device.

> ## ⚠️ Important: the two new tools are UNTESTED
> **Alchemist's Lab and Cook are brand new and have not been play-tested yet.** They load real Keizaal recipe data and the maths *looks* right, but the numbers haven't been checked against actual in-game play. Treat their prices as a starting point, double-check anything you're relying on, and please report anything that looks off. The three original tools (Smithy, Tailor, Traders) are unaffected by this and continue as before.

---

## 📦 Traders: bulk item import

The **Traders** tool got a bulk **item importer** (Settings tab). Instead of adding tradeable goods one at a time, you can now pull them in by the category: filter by material tier, tick the weapon/armor/misc groups you want in a collapsible tree, and add the lot in one click. Existing goods and prices are left untouched, and imported goods start at price 0 for you to fill in on the Market tab.

---

## ⚡ Everything's faster

The tools used to hang for about a second after almost every click — sorting the database, adding to an order, even just switching tabs. **That lag is gone.** The app now only redraws the panel you're actually looking at, and the cost engine was rebuilt so it stops recomputing the same recipe trees over and over. In practice, the big Smithy database now redraws in a fraction of a second instead of ~1 second, and most clicks feel instant.

---

## 🔀 One login, five workshops

From the strip at the top you can jump straight between all five tools without re-entering anything. Access codes can cover a single workshop or several at once, so a combined code opens exactly the tools it's meant to.
